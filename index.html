02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

At home at the intersection
of hardware & software.

I’m Aaron Sun, an electrical engineering undergraduate at the University of Waterloo. I’m interested in how physical systems work—and how to make them work better with code.

My experience spans electrical design at Plan Group, PCB rework and testing at Avnan, and semiconductor validation at New Radio Tech. I bring that hands-on perspective to digital design—from writing RTL to checking that it behaves as intended.
